Why is my model unpriced?

Retained strict policy or unsupported routing

Auto mode estimates unknown routing using Anthropic model rates and unknown cache-write duration using the 5-minute rate. Existing strict receipts are preserved until explicitly repriced:

claude-metrics pricing apply --data-dir <your-data-directory> --cost-mode auto --reprice

Old receipts and original evidence are retained. Estimates are not a subscription invoice. New dashboard runs use auto mode by default; --cost-mode strict disables these assumptions for new requests. Known other providers and genuinely missing rates remain unpriced.
